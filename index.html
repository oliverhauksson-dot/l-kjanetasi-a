Pick a cabinet. Save your score to see your place, and make the top 100 to get your name on the leaderboard.

About Pocket Arcade

Pocket Arcade is a collection of 13 classic-style games you can play for free in your browser, on a phone or a computer. There is nothing to download and no account to make. Your best score for each game is saved on your own device.

New to a game? Open it and the start screen tells you the controls. On a phone you can swipe, tap and drag; on a computer use the arrow keys, Space and the mouse.

How to play: Snake · Ski Runner · Breakout · Memory · Pong · Snap Grid · Invaders · Flappy · Maze Muncher · Blackjack · Cube Rush · Police Chase · Endless Ski

Questions

Are the games on Pocket Arcade free?

Yes. All 11 games are free to play, with no download, no account and no in-game purchases.

Do the games work on phones?

Yes. Every game works on phones and tablets with touch controls (tap, swipe and drag), and on computers with the keyboard and mouse.

Where are my scores saved?

Your best score for each game is saved in your own browser on your device. Clearing your browser data resets it.

Is Blackjack played with real money?

No. Blackjack on Pocket Arcade uses free play coins only. They have no value and can't be bought.

Which games can I play?

Snake, Ski Runner, Breakout, Memory, Pong, Snap Grid, Invaders, Flappy, Maze Muncher, Blackjack, Cube Rush, Police Chase and Endless Ski.
