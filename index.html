About Pocket Arcade

Pocket Arcade is a collection of 17 classic-style games you can play for free in your browser, on a phone or a computer. There is nothing to download and no account to make. Your best score for each game is saved on your own device.

New to a game? Open it and the start screen tells you the controls. On a phone you can swipe, tap and drag; on a computer use the arrow keys, Space and the mouse.

Questions

Are the games on Pocket Arcade free?

Yes. All 17 games are free to play, with no download, no account and no in-game purchases.

Do the games work on phones?

Yes. Every game works on phones and tablets with touch controls (tap, swipe and drag), and on computers with the keyboard and mouse.

Where are my scores saved?

Your best score for each game is saved in your own browser on your device. Clearing your browser data resets it.

What is the Daily Challenge?

Every day one game is the Daily Challenge. It starts the same way for everyone, and it has its own top 10 that starts empty again at midnight (UTC).

Can I add Pocket Arcade to my home screen?

Yes. Tap Add to home screen on the front page (on iPhone: the Share button, then Add to Home Screen). It then opens like an app, and the games you have played work offline too.

Which games can I play?

Snake, Ski Runner, Breakout, Memory, Pong, Snap Grid, Invaders, Sleigh Ride, Maze Muncher, Cube Rush, Police Chase, Endless Ski, Tower Defense, Fishing, Stack Tower, Minigolf and Word Guesser.
