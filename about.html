About Pocket Arcade

Pocket Arcade is a small website with 11 games that run right in your browser. They are free to play, need no download or account, and work on phones, tablets and computers.

The games

  • Snake: eat the fruit and grow longer without hitting the walls or your own tail.
  • Ski Runner: ski down an endless zig-zag mountain, jump the icebergs and trees, and fly off a ramp at every turn.
  • Breakout: bounce the ball off your paddle and clear every brick. Each new wall is faster.
  • Memory: flip cards two at a time and find all 8 pairs in as few moves as you can.
  • Pong: the classic paddle duel against the computer. The match ends when it reaches 5.
  • Blocks: turn and stack falling pieces. Full rows disappear and the pace picks up every 10 rows.
  • Invaders: defend the ground from waves of aliens while dodging their shots.
  • Flappy: tap to flap through the gaps between pipes. One touch and it's over.
  • Maze Muncher: eat every dot in the maze while ghosts chase you. Big dots let you chase them back.
  • Blackjack: beat the dealer to 21 with free play coins. No real money is involved.
  • Endless Ski: carve down a never-ending 3D mountain, dodge trees and rocks, and ski from day into a night with northern lights.

Scores

Your best score for each game is saved in your own browser. Clearing your browser data resets it.
