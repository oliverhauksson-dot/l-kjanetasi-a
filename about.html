About Pocket Arcade

Pocket Arcade is a small website with 17 retro-style games that run right in your browser. They are free to play, need no download or account, and work on phones, tablets and computers.

The games

  • Snake: eat the fruit and grow longer without hitting the walls or your own tail.
  • Ski Runner: ski down an endless zig-zag mountain, jump the icebergs and trees, and fly off a ramp at every turn.
  • Breakout: bounce the ball off your paddle and clear every brick. Each new wall is faster.
  • Memory: flip cards two at a time and find all 8 pairs in as few moves as you can.
  • Pong: the classic paddle duel against the computer. The match ends when it reaches 5.
  • Snap Grid: drag pieces onto an 8x8 grid. Full rows and columns clear, and the game ends when nothing fits.
  • Invaders: defend the ground from waves of aliens while dodging their shots.
  • Sleigh Ride: tap to fly Santa's sleigh between the chimneys and the icicles. One bump and the ride is over.
  • Maze Muncher: eat every dot in the maze while ghosts chase you. Big dots let you chase them back.
  • Cube Rush: a one-button jumping game. Jump the spikes and blocks in 3 levels, or see how far you get in Endless.
  • Police Chase: drive a getaway car round the city and don't let the police catch you. Make them crash into each other.
  • Endless Ski: ski an endless 3D terrain park, grind rails, fly off a 10 m mega jump, and ski from day into a night with northern lights.
  • Tower Defense: build arrow, cannon, ice and zap towers along a winding path and stop the monster waves before they reach your castle.
  • Fishing: cast your line, wait for a bite and reel in rare fish before the sun sets, without snapping the line.
  • Stack Tower: drop sliding blocks on top of each other. Whatever hangs over the edge is cut off, so line them up to build high.
  • Minigolf: nine holes of walls, sand, water, bumpers and spinning windmill bars. Finish the round in as few shots as you can.
  • Word Guesser: guess the secret 5-letter word in 6 tries, then the next one. Fewer guesses give more points.

Scores

Your best score for each game is saved in your own browser. Clearing your browser data resets it.
