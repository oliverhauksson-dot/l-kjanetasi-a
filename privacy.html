Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

What we collect

Pocket Arcade has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details.

Scores and settings

Your best scores and your sound setting are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are not sent to us or anyone else. You can delete them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Advertising and cookies

If this site shows ads, they are served by third-party vendors, including Google. These vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the site's GitHub page.
