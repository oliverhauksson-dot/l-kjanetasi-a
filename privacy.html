Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

What we collect

Pocket Arcade has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details.

Scores and settings

Your best scores and your sound setting are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. Your saved best scores stay on your device. The score of each finished game is counted in our visitor statistics, without your name or any account. You can delete them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Visitor statistics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which games are played and what scores are reached. It records things like the pages you view, your rough location, and your device and browser type, and may set cookies to tell repeat visits apart. These cookies are only set if you accept cookies. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Advertising and cookies

This site shows ads from Google AdSense. Ads are served by third-party vendors, including Google. These vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, you are asked for your consent before personalised ads or advertising cookies are used. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy settings link shown with the consent message.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the site's GitHub page.
