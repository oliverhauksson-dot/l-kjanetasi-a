About Pong

Pong is the classic paddle game. You are the left paddle and play against the computer. Score as many points as you can before the computer reaches 5.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser.

Tips

  • Hit the ball near the end of your paddle to change its angle.
  • Move back to the middle after every hit.
  • The computer is fast but not perfect, so aim for the corners.
  • Press P to pause.

How to play

  • Move your paddle to hit the ball back past the computer.
  • You are the left paddle. You get a point when the computer misses.
  • Score as many points as you can before the computer gets 5.

Controls

Arrow keys or mouse on a computer, drag on a phone.
