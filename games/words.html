About Word Guesser

Word Guesser is a word puzzle game. Type any 5-letter English word as a guess. The tiles then show how close you are: green means the right letter in the right place, gold means the letter is in the word but in another place, and dark means the letter is not in the word at all. Solve the word and a new one comes. See how many words you can solve in a row.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser, and there is a top 10 for this week and for all time.

Tips

  • Start with a word that has several different vowels.
  • Don't use dark letters again: they are not in the word.
  • A gold letter is in the word, so try it in a new place.
  • The keyboard shows the colour of every letter you've tried.

How to play

  • Type a 5-letter word and press Enter.
  • Green: right letter, right place. Gold: in the word, wrong place. Dark: not in the word.
  • Solve the word in 6 guesses to get the next one. Fewer guesses give more points.
  • Miss a word and the game is over.

Controls

Type on the keyboard and press Enter, or tap the letters on the screen.

Questions

How is the score counted?

A word solved in 1 guess is 60 points, in 2 guesses 50, and so on down to 10 points in 6 guesses.

Which words can I guess?

Any real 5-letter English word. The secret words are common everyday words.

What happens when I miss a word?

The game shows you the word and the run is over. Your score is the points from all the words you solved.
