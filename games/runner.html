About Ski Runner

Ski Runner is an endless skiing game. Your skier heads down a zig-zag mountain on their own, and every slope is different. You jump the icebergs and trees, and at the end of each slope you fly off a ramp, turn round in the air and land on the slope below. After 400 m, snowballs start falling from the sky.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser.

Tips

  • Tap or press quickly for a short hop, and hold for a higher jump.
  • Watch for a shadow on the snow: that's where a falling snowball will land.
  • Trees and wide icebergs need a longer jump.
  • Watch further ahead as the game speeds up, so you can time jumps.
  • Press P to pause.

How to play

  • Your skier goes down the slope on their own. Jump to clear the icebergs and trees.
  • Hold jump to go higher. Trees and wide icebergs need a longer jump.
  • At the end of each slope you fly off the ramp, turn round in the air, and land on the next slope going the other way.
  • The further you go, the faster and steeper it gets, the slopes get shorter, and snowballs start rolling down the slope ahead of you.
  • After 400 m snowballs drop out of the sky. Watch for their shadow on the snow and don't be under one when it lands.
  • Your score is how far you get.

Controls

Space on a computer, tap on a phone.

Questions

How do I jump higher in Ski Runner?

Hold Space or keep your finger down. A quick tap is a short hop, and holding gives a higher, longer jump for trees and wide icebergs.

What are the shadows on the snow?

After 400 m, snowballs fall from the sky. The shadow shows where one will land, so don't be under it.

Does Ski Runner ever end?

No. The mountain never ends, but it gets faster and steeper the further you go. Your score is how far you get.
