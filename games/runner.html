Ski Runner

Ski down an endless zig-zag mountain, jump the icebergs and trees, and fly off a ramp at every turn. It gets faster, steeper and tighter the further you go, and after 500 m snowballs fall from the sky. Free to play in your browser, with no download and no account.

▶ PLAY SKI RUNNER

How to play

  • Your skier goes down the slope on its own. Jump to clear the icebergs and trees.
  • Hold jump to go higher. Trees and wide icebergs need a longer jump.
  • At the end of each slope you fly off the ramp, turn round in the air, and land on the next slope going the other way.
  • The further you go, the faster and steeper it gets, the slopes get shorter, and snowballs start rolling up at you.
  • After 500 m snowballs drop out of the sky. Watch for their shadow on the snow and don't be under one when it lands.
  • Your score is how far you get.

Controls

Space on a computer, tap on a phone.
