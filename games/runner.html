Runner

Jump over blocks while the speed keeps climbing. Birds join in after 400 m. Free to play in your browser, with no download and no account.

▶ PLAY RUNNER

How to play

  • Your runner moves on its own. Jump to clear the blocks in the way.
  • Hold jump to go higher. The game speeds up the further you go.
  • After 400 m birds fly in low. Stay on the ground and let them pass over you.
  • Your score is how far you get.

Controls

Space on a computer, tap on a phone.
