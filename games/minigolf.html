About Minigolf

Minigolf is a 3D mini golf game with 9 holes and a camera right behind your ball. Pull back and let go to putt, like a slingshot. Roll up ramps, jump a pond, time your putt through a turning windmill, bounce off glowing pinball bumpers, cross a drawbridge, go round a loop-the-loop, slide over an ice rink and climb a volcano. Most holes have a risky shortcut, and some holes have a breeze that pushes the ball while it is in the air. You play each hole at the same time as 3 bots, and a scorecard after every hole shows who is ahead. In December the course gets a Christmas look. Your score is your total number of shots, so fewer is better.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser, and there is a top 10 for this week and for all time.

Tips

  • Pull back only a little for short putts. A long pull is a hard hit.
  • Look for the shortcuts, but they are risky: a jump that is too hard can fly off the course.
  • Wait for the windmill door and the drawbridge before you putt.
  • On a windy hole, aim a little into the wind before a jump.
  • Tap the eye button or press C to look at the whole hole.
  • Each hole stops at 7 shots, so a bad hole can't ruin the whole round.

How to play

  • Press anywhere, pull back and let go. Slide sideways while you pull to aim. The dots show where the ball will go and how hard.
  • Sink the ball in the cup. A ball that comes in too fast hops over it.
  • Water or a ball that falls off the course costs one shot, and the ball goes back to where you hit it from.
  • After 9 holes your score is your total number of shots. Beat the 3 bots to win the round.

Controls

Drag and let go with the mouse or a finger. On a computer you can also aim with the left and right arrows, set the power with up and down, putt with Space, and press C to look at the whole hole.

Questions

How is the score counted?

Your score is the total number of shots for all 9 holes, so a lower score is better. Par for the course is 26.

Who are the other players?

Three computer bots with made-up gamer names. They play each hole at the same time as you, and their balls are see-through so they never get in your way.

What happens in the water?

The ball goes back to where you hit it from, and you get one extra shot. The same happens if the ball falls off the course.

Is there a shot limit?

Yes. A hole stops at 7 shots and you move on to the next one.
