About Minigolf

Minigolf is a top-down mini golf game with 9 holes. Pull back and let go to putt the ball, like a slingshot. Bounce it off the wooden walls and bumpers, get past the spinning windmill bars, and stay out of the water. Sand slows the ball down, and water costs a shot and puts the ball back where it was. Your score is the total number of shots for the whole round, so fewer is better.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser, and there is a top 10 for this week and for all time.

Tips

  • Pull back only a little for short putts. A long pull is a hard hit.
  • Use the walls: a bank shot is often easier than a straight one.
  • Time your putt past the windmill bar.
  • Each hole stops at 7 shots, so a bad hole can't ruin the whole round.
  • Press P to pause.

How to play

  • Press anywhere, pull back and let go. The dots show where the ball will go and how hard.
  • Sink the ball in the cup. A ball that comes in too fast can roll over it.
  • After 9 holes your score is the total number of shots. Fewer shots is better.

Controls

Drag and let go with the mouse or a finger. On a computer you can also aim with the left and right arrows, set the power with up and down, and putt with Space.

Questions

How is the score counted?

Your score is the total number of shots for all 9 holes, so a lower score is better. Par for the course is 26.

What happens in the water?

The ball goes back to where you hit it from, and you get one extra shot.

Is there a shot limit?

Yes. A hole stops at 7 shots and you move on to the next one.
