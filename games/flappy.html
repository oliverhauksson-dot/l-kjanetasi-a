About Flappy

Flappy is a one-button game. Tap to make the bird flap up and fly through the gaps between pipes. Touch a pipe or the ground and the flight is over.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser.

Tips

  • Tap in a steady rhythm instead of many quick taps.
  • Aim for the middle of each gap.
  • Each pipe you pass is one point.
  • Press P to pause.

How to play

  • Each tap makes the bird flap up. Let go and it falls.
  • Fly through the gaps between the pipes.
  • Touching a pipe or the ground ends the run. Each pipe you pass is a point.

Controls

Space on a computer, tap on a phone.
