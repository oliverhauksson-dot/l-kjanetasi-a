About Sleigh Ride

Sleigh Ride is a one-button Christmas game. Santa's sleigh flies over a snowy town at night. Tap to make it fly up, and steer through the gaps between the brick chimneys below and the icicles above. Every chimney you pass is a point, and a present drops down it. Bump into anything or touch the snow and the ride is over.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser, and there is a top 10 for this week and for all time.

Tips

  • Tap in a steady rhythm instead of many quick taps.
  • Aim for the middle of each gap.
  • The sleigh speeds up a little with every chimney.
  • Press P to pause.

How to play

  • Each tap makes the sleigh fly up. Let go and it sinks.
  • Fly through the gaps between the chimneys and the icicles.
  • Touching a chimney, an icicle or the snow ends the ride. Each chimney you pass is a point.

Controls

Space or the up arrow on a computer, tap on a phone.

Questions

How do I get better at Sleigh Ride?

Tap in a steady rhythm instead of many quick taps, and aim for the middle of each gap.

Each chimney you fly past is 1 point. Touching a chimney, an icicle or the snow ends the ride.
