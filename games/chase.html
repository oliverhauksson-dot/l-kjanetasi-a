About Police Chase

Police Chase is a car chase game seen from above. You drive the yellow getaway car through a 3D city of skyscrapers, apartment buildings, shops and parks, and police cars with flashing lights hunt you down. The longer you get away, the more police join the chase and the faster they drive.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser.

How to play

  • Drive round the city and keep away from the police cars.
  • If a police car touches you, you are busted. You have 3 lives, shown as hearts.
  • After you are busted you blink for a moment and can't be caught.
  • Police cars that crash into each other spin out and stop for a few seconds, and you get 100 bonus points.
  • Police cars that hit a building hard also stop for a while.
  • Grab coins for 50 points. The blue lightning gives you a speed boost for 3 seconds.
  • The stars at the top show how many police are after you. Red and blue arrows at the edge point to police you can't see yet.

Scoring

You get 10 points for every second you get away, plus 50 for each coin and 100 for each police crash.

Tips

  • The police turn slower than you. Take sharp corners to lose them.
  • Lead two police cars towards each other, then turn away at the last moment.
  • Save the speed boost for a long straight road.
  • Press P to pause.

Controls

Arrow keys or WASD on a computer. On a phone, put your thumb anywhere on the game and drag the way you want to drive.
