About Maze Muncher

Maze Muncher is a maze chase game. Eat every dot in the maze while four ghosts chase you. Big dots turn the ghosts blue for a few seconds so you can chase them back. You have 3 lives.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser.

Tips

  • Save the big dots until ghosts are close, then turn and chase them.
  • Catching several blue ghosts in a row doubles the bonus each time.
  • Use the tunnel at the sides to get away. Ghosts are slower in it.
  • Press P to pause.

How to play

  • Move through the maze and eat all the dots to clear the level.
  • Ghosts chase you. Touch one and you lose a life. You have 3.
  • Eat a big orange dot and the ghosts turn blue for a few seconds. Catch them for bonus points.
  • Each new level is a bit faster. Use the tunnel at the sides to escape.

Controls

Arrow keys or WASD on a computer, swipe on a phone.

Questions

What do the big dots do?

Eating a big orange dot turns the ghosts blue for a few seconds. Catch blue ghosts for bonus points, and each one in a row doubles the bonus.

How do I escape the ghosts?

Use the tunnel at the sides of the maze. It takes you to the other side, and ghosts are slower in it.

What happens when I eat all the dots?

You clear the level and a new one starts, a bit faster than the last.
