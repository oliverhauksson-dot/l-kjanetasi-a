Blocks

Turn and stack falling pieces. Full rows disappear and the pace picks up every 10 rows. Free to play in your browser, with no download and no account.

▶ PLAY BLOCKS

How to play

  • Move and turn the falling pieces to fit them together.
  • A full row clears and gives you points.
  • Every 10 rows the pieces fall faster. The game ends when the stack reaches the top.

Controls

Arrow keys on a computer (Up turns, Space drops). On a phone, drag to move and tap to turn.
