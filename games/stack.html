About Stack Tower

Stack Tower is a one-button stacking game. A block slides back and forth above your tower. Tap to drop it. Whatever hangs over the edge of the block below is cut off and falls away, so the tower gets narrower with every miss. Drop a block right on top for a perfect: nothing is cut, and three perfects in a row make the block wider again. Miss the tower completely and the game is over.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser, and there is a top 10 for this week and for all time.

Tips

  • Watch the edge of the block below, not the moving block.
  • Perfect drops in a row make your blocks wider again.
  • The blocks get faster as the tower grows, so take your time early on.
  • Press P to pause.

How to play

  • A block slides from side to side above the tower.
  • Tap to drop it. The part that hangs over the edge is cut off.
  • Every block you place is one point. Miss the tower and the game is over.

Controls

Space or the down arrow on a computer, tap on a phone.

Questions

How is the score counted?

Every block you place on the tower is 1 point.

What is a perfect?

A block dropped almost exactly on top of the one below. Nothing is cut off, and three in a row make the block a little wider.

Does it get harder?

Yes. The blocks slide faster the higher the tower gets.
