Cube Rush

Cube Rush is a one-button jumping game. Your cube slides along on its own, and you jump over spikes and onto blocks. Pick Level 1, 2 or 3, or play Endless, which keeps getting faster. Free to play in your browser, with no download and no account.

About Cube Rush

When you open Cube Rush you pick a level. Level 1 is easy, Level 2 is medium and Level 3 is hard. Each one takes about 50 seconds if you don't crash. A bar at the top shows how far you got, and your best % for each level is saved. Endless never stops: it gets faster and harder the longer you go, and the colours change every 200 m.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best % for each level, and your best Endless distance, are saved in your browser.

How to play

  • Your cube moves on its own. Jump to get over the spikes.
  • You can land on top of blocks, but crashing into the side of one ends the run.
  • Yellow jump pads on the floor bounce you up high.
  • Yellow rings float in the air. Tap while you touch one to jump again in mid-air.
  • In a level, crashing starts it again from the beginning. In Endless, your score is how far you get.

Tips

  • Hold jump to keep jumping every time you land. It's great for long rows of spikes and blocks.
  • Three spikes in a row need a jump right at the edge of the first one.
  • Press M or tap the ☰ button to go back to the level picker. Press P to pause.

Controls

Space, Up arrow or click on a computer. Tap on a phone.

Questions

How many levels does Cube Rush have?

Three levels (easy, medium and hard), each about 50 seconds long, plus Endless mode, which never stops and keeps getting faster.

What are the yellow rings?

Tap while your cube touches a yellow ring in the air to jump again. Yellow pads on the floor bounce you up high on their own.

Can I land on the blocks?

Yes, you can land on top of a block. Crashing into the side of one ends the run.
