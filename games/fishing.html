About Fishing

Fishing is a one-button game on a quiet lake. You stand on a wooden dock with your rod. Cast your line, choose how deep the hook sinks, and wait for a fish to bite. Then reel it in without snapping the line. A round lasts 60 seconds, and the sun sets while you fish.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser, and every fish you have ever caught goes in your fish book.

Tips

  • Small fish swim near the top. The big and rare ones live deeper, so let the hook sink.
  • The float bobs a little when a fish nibbles. Wait until it goes right under, then press.
  • Press fast when it bites for a perfect hook and 25% extra points.
  • While reeling, tap and hold in short bursts to keep the marker in the green.
  • When the fish pulls hard, let go for a moment, or the line snaps.
  • Nothing biting? Hold to wind your line back in and cast somewhere else.
  • Leave your hook on the bottom next to the old boot and you will catch it. It is worth 0 points!
  • Press P to pause.

How to play

  • Hold to swing the rod back. The power bar goes up and down: let go to cast. More power casts further.
  • The hook sinks after it lands. Tap to stop it at the depth you want, or let it sink all the way to the bottom.
  • Wait for a fish. When the float goes under and a red ! appears, press right away to hook it.
  • Hold to reel the fish in. Holding makes the line tighter, letting go makes it looser. Keep the marker in the green part of the bar.
  • Too tight (red) for too long and the line snaps. Too loose for too long and the fish gets away.
  • When the time runs out, you can still land the fish that is on your line.

The fish

  • Minnow: 10 points. Small and easy, near the top.
  • Perch: 25 points. Striped, near the top.
  • Trout: 50 points. Middle of the lake.
  • Salmon: 80 points. Deeper down.
  • Pike: 120 points. Long, fast and strong.
  • Catfish: 150 points. Heavy, right at the bottom.
  • Golden Fish: 400 points. Very rare, very fast, and it sparkles.
  • Lake Shark: 1000 points. Super rare and huge, deep down. Good luck!
  • Old Boot: 0 points. Oops.

Bigger fish of the same kind are worth a few more points.

Controls

On a computer: hold Space to cast, tap Space to stop the hook and to hook a fish, hold Space to reel. On a phone: do the same by touching the screen.

Questions

How do I catch the rare fish?

Let the hook sink deep. Golden fish swim in the middle of the lake, and the Lake Shark and catfish live near the bottom. They bite fast and pull hard, so watch the tension bar.

Why did my line snap?

You held on while the line was too tight. When the bar goes into the red, or the fish pulls hard, let go for a moment and then reel again.

How is the score counted?

Every fish is worth points: bigger and rarer fish are worth more, and a perfect hook gives 25% extra. A round lasts 60 seconds.
