About Invaders

Invaders is a retro space shooter. Move your cannon left and right and shoot down waves of aliens before they land, while dodging their shots. You have 3 ships.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser.

Tips

  • Clear one side of the alien group first so they take longer to reach the edge.
  • Keep moving so their shots are harder to land.
  • The aliens speed up as there are fewer of them.
  • Press P to pause.

How to play

  • Move your cannon left and right and shoot the aliens.
  • Dodge their shots and stop them before they reach the ground. You have 3 ships.
  • Clear a wave and a new one comes in.

Controls

Arrow keys and Space on a computer, drag and tap on a phone.
