Blackjack

Play blackjack against the dealer with free play chips. Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Free to play in your browser, with no download and no account.

▶ PLAY BLACKJACK

How to play

  • You start with 100 play chips. They are not real money and can't be bought.
  • Pick a bet and press Deal. Number cards count as their number, J, Q and K count as 10, and an Ace is 1 or 11.
  • Hit to take another card or Stand to keep your hand. Double doubles your bet for exactly one more card.
  • Go over 21 and you lose. The dealer stands on 17. Blackjack pays 3 to 2.
  • Your best is the most chips you ever had. Cash out any time to end the game.

Controls

Click or tap the buttons, or press H to hit, S to stand, D to double and Enter to deal.
