About Blackjack

Blackjack is the classic card game against the dealer, played with free play coins only. Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. No real money is used and coins can't be bought.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser.

Tips

  • Stand on 17 or more, and usually hit on 11 or less.
  • The dealer must hit until 17, so a weak dealer card often means the dealer busts.
  • Win 3 hands in a row for 25 bonus coins.
  • Cash out when you're ahead to lock in your best score.

How to play

  • You start with 100 play coins. They are not real money, can't be bought, and every new game starts again at 100.
  • Pick a bet (10, 25, 50 or all in) and press Deal. Number cards count as their number, J, Q and K count as 10, and an Ace is 1 or 11.
  • Hit to take another card or Stand to keep your hand. If your first two cards have the same value, you can Split them into two hands with a second bet.
  • Go over 21 and you lose. The dealer stands on 17. Blackjack pays 3 to 2.
  • Win 3 hands in a row for 25 bonus coins. Your stats show hands played, wins, losses and your biggest win.
  • Your best is the most coins you ever had. Cash out any time to end the game.

Controls

Click or tap the buttons, or press H to hit, S to stand, X to split and Enter to deal.
