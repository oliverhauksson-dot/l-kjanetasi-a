About Snake

Snake is the classic arcade game where you steer a growing snake around the board. Every fruit you eat makes the snake longer, so the board gets more crowded the better you do.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser.

Tips

  • Stay near the middle early on so you have room to turn.
  • Don't chase every fruit straight on. Plan a path that leaves you a way out.
  • The snake speeds up a little with every fruit, so turn early.
  • Press P to pause.

How to play

  • Steer the snake toward the fruit. Each bite makes it longer and adds to your score.
  • Hitting a wall or your own tail ends the game.
  • Plan ahead: the longer you get, the less room you have.

Controls

Arrow keys or WASD on a computer, swipe on a phone.
