Snake

Eat the fruit and grow longer without hitting the walls or your own tail. Free to play in your browser, with no download and no account.

▶ PLAY SNAKE

How to play

  • Steer the snake toward the fruit. Each bite makes it longer and adds to your score.
  • Hitting a wall or your own tail ends the game.
  • Plan ahead: the longer you get, the less room you have.

Controls

Arrow keys or WASD on a computer, swipe on a phone.
