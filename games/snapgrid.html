About Snap Grid

Snap Grid is a block puzzle. You get three pieces at a time and place them anywhere on the grid. Fill a whole row or column to clear it and score points. There is no timer, so take your time.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser.

Tips

  • Clearing several lines with one piece gives far more points than one at a time.
  • Keep clearing at least once every 3 pieces to build a combo, which multiplies your bonus.
  • Leave room for the big 3x3 square and the long 5 block lines.
  • Empty the whole board for a 300 point bonus.

How to play

  • Drag one of the three pieces under the grid onto an empty spot.
  • A full row or column clears and gives you points. Blocks that will clear light up while you hold a piece.
  • When all three pieces are placed you get three new ones. Pieces can't be turned.
  • The game ends when none of your pieces fit anywhere. Pieces that don't fit are shown faded.

Controls

Drag with your finger or mouse. On a keyboard, press 1, 2 or 3 to pick a piece, move it with the arrow keys and press Space to place it.
