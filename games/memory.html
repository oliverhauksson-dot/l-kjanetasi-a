Memory

Flip cards two at a time and find all 8 pairs in as few moves as you can. Free to play in your browser, with no download and no account.

▶ PLAY MEMORY

How to play

  • Flip two cards. If they match, they stay face up.
  • If not, they turn back over, so try to remember where they were.
  • Find all 8 pairs. Fewer moves is a better score.

Controls

Click or tap a card.
