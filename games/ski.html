About Endless Ski

Endless Ski is a real 3D downhill skiing game. You race down a mountain valley that never ends, seen from just behind your skier, with forest and rocky peaks on both sides. The valley bends left and right, rollers and kickers throw you into the air, and it gets faster the further you go. Keep going long enough and the sun sets, the stars come out and the northern lights dance over the mountains. Between the terrain parks you ski through a slalom, across a frozen lake, into a snowstorm or away from an avalanche.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser.

Tips

  • The valley bends under you, so steer a little into every bend.
  • Skiing up the valley sides slows you down, and the forest there is full of trees.
  • Look far ahead and pick your line through the trees early, because it gets fast.
  • In the air, hold left or right to spin. Land facing downhill or straight backwards, or it gets sketchy and you lose speed and your boost.
  • Land tricks back to back: every clean landing before the boost fades adds more speed on top.
  • A 180 off a hop on flat snow is the easiest boost. Save flips and big spins for the kickers, where they give the biggest boost.
  • Hop off the end of a rail with Space and spin out, for a boost from the rail and another from the spin.
  • You can hop any time, even on bumpy snow. Press Space just before you land and you hop again straight away.
  • More speed means the trees come at you faster, so look further ahead while you are boosted.
  • A flip has to come all the way round to your feet. Land upside down and the run is over.
  • A jump on flat snow is just long enough for a 180 or one flip. Save 360s, 540s and double flips for the kickers.
  • Press P to pause.

How to play

  • Your skier goes down the mountain on their own. Steer left and right to dodge the trees and rocks.
  • Every metre you ski is 1 point, and every coin is 10.
  • Press Space to jump, anywhere on the snow or off a rail. Kickers and rollers throw you higher. Air time scores points, and your skier pulls a grab like an Indy or a Method on every big jump.
  • In the air, hold left or right to spin. A 180 is 100 points, a 360 is 200, a 540 is 350, a 720 is 500 and so on. Hold W for a front flip or S for a backflip, 300 points each.
  • Land a 180 (or a 540) and you ride away backwards, called switch. Tricks you take off for while riding switch score half as much again. Spin another 180 to face forward again.
  • Every 1150 m or so you ride through a 520 m terrain park, with four lanes full of rails, rainbow rails, kinked rails, boxes, kickers and side hits. Pop off the little ramp onto a rail, or ride straight onto a box, and slide along it for points. Hold left or right to hop off early, or press Space to pop off and spin out. Slide backwards for half as many points again, and press S on a rail to spin round (a switch-up) for 75 points. Ride into the side of a rail and it knocks you aside and slows you down.
  • Side hits on the edges of the park throw you back towards the middle. Steer into a wooden wall to ride up it for a wallride.
  • In the middle of the park there is a jump line of three kickers that get bigger and bigger, and a gap jump: clear the gap and land on the deck for 300 points, or come up short and you case it and lose speed.
  • At the end of the park is the mega jump, with a crowd cheering. Big air time gives a big bonus, so save your biggest spins and flips for it.
  • Hit park features one after another for a line bonus. At the end of the park you see your park points and your best line.
  • Press C (or tap SKIN) to change your skier's outfit. There are 8 to pick from, and your choice is saved.
  • Every clean trick gives you a speed boost that fades over a few seconds. Bigger tricks and a higher multiplier give a bigger boost, and sliding a rail keeps your speed and boosts you when you come off.
  • Each clean landing raises your multiplier, up to x5. Land another trick before the bar runs out to keep it, but a sketchy landing drops it back to x1.
  • It gets faster and busier the further you go.
  • Between the terrain parks comes a special stretch, in a different order every run:
    • Slalom: ski between the two poles of every gate. Each gate in a row is worth more, and all of them is a perfect slalom for 1000 points.
    • Frozen lake: the ice is fast and slippery, so you slide on sideways and turns take a long time. Only rocks stick out of it.
    • Snowstorm: you can't see far, and gusts of wind push you left and right.
    • Avalanche: it chases you down the mountain and gets faster. Keep your speed up with tricks and don't ski up the sides. Reach the end and you escape for 500 points.
  • Reindeer and hares wait at the edge of the forest and run across when you come close. Jump over a hare for 150 points, or a reindeer for 400 if you hop high enough.
  • Hit a tree, a rock or a reindeer and the run is over.

Controls

On a computer: left and right arrow keys (or A and D) to steer, Space to jump. In the air, hold left or right to spin, W (or up) to front flip and S (or down) to back flip. On a rail, S spins you round.

On a phone: hold the left or right side of the screen to steer and swipe up to jump. In the air, swipe up for a front flip or down for a backflip, and keep your finger down to keep flipping. Swipe down on a rail to spin round.

Questions

How do I do tricks in Endless Ski?

Press Space (or swipe up on a phone) to jump. In the air, hold left or right to spin, W to front flip and S to back flip. A 180 lands you riding backwards (switch), and tricks done switch score half as much again. Clean landings raise your multiplier up to x5.

What is the terrain park?

About every 1150 m you ski through a big terrain park with rails, rainbow rails, kinked rails, boxes, kickers, wall rides, a gap jump and a mega jump. Slide along rails and boxes for points.

Does Endless Ski work on phones?

Yes. Hold the left or right side of the screen to steer. It is a real 3D game, so it runs best on a newer phone.
