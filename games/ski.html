About Endless Ski

Endless Ski is a 3D downhill skiing game. You race down a mountain that never ends, seen from just behind your skier. The piste bends left and right and rolls over bumps, and it gets faster the further you go. Keep going long enough and the sun sets, the stars come out and the northern lights dance over the mountains.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser.

Tips

  • The piste bends under you, so steer a little into every bend to stay on it.
  • Deep snow off the piste slows you down, and the forest is full of trees.
  • There is always a gap in a row of trees and rocks. Look ahead and pick it early.
  • Hit a snow ramp for big air. Stay in the air long enough and you spin a 360 for extra points.
  • Press P to pause.

How to play

  • Your skier goes down the mountain on their own. Steer left and right to dodge trees, rocks and other skiers.
  • Grab coins for 10 points each. Every metre you ski is 1 point.
  • Snow ramps throw you into the air, over anything low. A big air is worth 50 points, and a 360 spin 100.
  • It gets faster, narrower and busier the further you go.
  • Hit anything and the run is over.

Controls

Left and right arrow keys (or A and D) on a computer. On a phone, hold the left or right side of the screen.
