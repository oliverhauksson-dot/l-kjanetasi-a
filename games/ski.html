About Endless Ski

Endless Ski is a real 3D downhill skiing game. You race down a mountain valley that never ends, seen from just behind your skier, with forest and rocky peaks on both sides. The valley bends left and right, rollers and kickers throw you into the air, and it gets faster the further you go. Keep going long enough and the sun sets, the stars come out and the northern lights dance over the mountains.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser.

Tips

  • The valley bends under you, so steer a little into every bend.
  • Skiing up the valley sides slows you down, and the forest there is full of trees.
  • Look far ahead and pick your line through the trees early, because it gets fast.
  • In the air, hold left or right to spin. Land facing downhill again, or it gets sketchy and you lose speed.
  • Press P to pause.

How to play

  • Your skier goes down the mountain on their own. Steer left and right to dodge the trees and rocks.
  • Every metre you ski is 1 point, and every coin is 10.
  • Kickers and rollers throw you into the air. Air time scores points, and every full 360 spin you land is 200. Your skier pulls a grab like an Indy or a Method on every big jump.
  • Every 900 m or so you ride through a terrain park, full of rails, boxes, kickers and side hits. Pop off the little ramp onto a rail, or ride straight onto a box, and slide along it for points. Hold left or right to hop off early. Ride into the side of a rail and it knocks you aside and slows you down.
  • Side hits on the edges of the park throw you back towards the middle.
  • Each clean landing raises your multiplier, up to x5. Land another trick before the bar runs out to keep it, but a sketchy landing drops it back to x1.
  • It gets faster and busier the further you go.
  • Hit a tree or a rock and the run is over.

Controls

Left and right arrow keys (or A and D) on a computer. On a phone, hold the left or right side of the screen. Hold a direction in the air to spin.
