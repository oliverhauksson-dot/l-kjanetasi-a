About Endless Ski

Endless Ski is a real 3D downhill skiing game in a terrain park that never ends, seen from just behind your skier. The park bends left and right down a mountain valley, with an orange safety fence, light towers, a chairlift and cheering fans along the sides, and it gets faster the further you go. Keep going long enough and the sun sets, the park lights come on, the stars come out and the northern lights dance over the mountains.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser.

Tips

  • The park bends under you, so steer a little into every bend and stay off the fence.
  • Look far ahead and pick your line early, because it gets fast.
  • In the air, hold left or right to spin. Land facing downhill or straight backwards, or it gets sketchy and you lose speed and your boost.
  • Land tricks back to back: every clean landing before the boost fades adds more speed on top.
  • A 180 off a hop on flat snow is the easiest boost. Save flips and big spins for the kickers, where they give the biggest boost.
  • Hop off the end of a rail with Space and spin out, for a boost from the rail and another from the spin.
  • You can hop any time, even on bumpy snow. Press Space just before you land and you hop again straight away.
  • More speed means longer jumps, so you fly further off every kicker while you are boosted.
  • A flip has to come all the way round to your feet. Land upside down and the run is over.
  • A jump on flat snow is just long enough for a 180 or one flip. Save 360s, 540s and double flips for the kickers.
  • Press P to pause.

How to play

  • Your skier goes down the park on their own. Steer left and right to pick your features.
  • Every metre you ski is 1 point, and every coin is 10.
  • Press Space to jump, anywhere on the snow or off a rail. Kickers and rollers throw you higher. Air time scores points, and your skier pulls a grab like an Indy or a Method on every big jump.
  • In the air, hold left or right to spin. A 180 is 100 points, a 360 is 200, a 540 is 350, a 720 is 500 and so on. Hold W for a front flip or S for a backflip, 300 points each.
  • Land a 180 (or a 540) and you ride away backwards, called switch. Tricks you take off for while riding switch score half as much again. Spin another 180 to face forward again.
  • Every run starts on the ski jump. You race down the in-run on your own, then press Space right at the edge to fly further. You get 15 points for every metre, 500 more past the red K line at 95 m and 1,500 more past the blue HS line at 110 m.
  • The ski village: ride the kicker in the middle up onto the first roof, then ski from roof to roof. The houses are different every time. Every roof is a step lower than the one before, so ski off the end and drop onto the next one: 250 points for every roof gap. Slide the rails along the roofs and the rail from one roof down to the next, and fly off the snow kickers for a roof drop bonus. Get across every roof without touching the snow for a ROOFTOP RUN, 2,500 points.
  • After the houses comes the town square, with a giant Christmas tree, an ice rink, market stalls and frozen fountains. Then comes the clock tower: ski through its arch for 300 points. Ski into a house, the church, a chimney, a lamp post, a snowman or anything in the square and the run is over.
  • The top speed is 200 km/h. Once you are there, tricks don't boost you any faster.
  • The park comes in sections, one after another. The rail garden has four lanes full of rails, rainbow rails, kinked rails, boxes, kickers and side hits. Pop off the little ramp onto a rail, or ride straight onto a box, and slide along it for points. Hold left or right to hop off early, or press Space to pop off and spin out. Slide backwards for half as many points again, and press S on a rail to spin round (a switch-up) for 75 points. Ride into the side of a rail and it knocks you aside and slows you down.
  • Side hits on the edges of the park throw you back towards the middle. Steer into a wooden wall to ride up it for a wallride.
  • The jump line has four kickers in a row that get bigger and bigger, up to 5.6 m. The gap jump has a gap and then a landing deck: clear it for 300 points, but come up short into the deck and the run is over.
  • After every round of sections comes the 10 m mega jump, with a big crowd and a DJ booth by the landing. Big air time gives a big bonus, so save your biggest spins and flips for it.
  • Hit park features one after another for a line bonus.
  • Every clean trick gives you a speed boost that fades over a few seconds. Bigger tricks and a higher multiplier give a bigger boost, and sliding a rail keeps your speed and boosts you when you come off.
  • Each clean landing raises your multiplier, up to x5. Land another trick before the bar runs out to keep it, but a sketchy landing drops it back to x1.
  • It gets faster and busier the further you go.
  • Land upside down, come up short on the gap jump, ski into a house or hit the safety fence and the run is over.

Controls

On a computer: left and right arrow keys (or A and D) to steer, Space to jump. In the air, hold left or right to spin, W (or up) to front flip and S (or down) to back flip. On a rail, S spins you round.

On a phone: hold the left or right side of the screen to steer and swipe up to jump. In the air, swipe up for a front flip or down for a backflip, and keep your finger down to keep flipping. Swipe down on a rail to spin round.

Questions

How do I do tricks in Endless Ski?

Press Space (or swipe up on a phone) to jump. In the air, hold left or right to spin, W to front flip and S to back flip. A 180 lands you riding backwards (switch), and tricks done switch score half as much again. Clean landings raise your multiplier up to x5.

What is the terrain park?

The whole run is one endless terrain park, built from sections one after another: a rail garden, a ski village, a jump line, a gap jump, wall rides and the mega jump. Slide along rails and boxes for points, and ski over the village rooftops.

Does Endless Ski work on phones?

Yes. Hold the left or right side of the screen to steer. It is a real 3D game, so it runs best on a newer phone.
