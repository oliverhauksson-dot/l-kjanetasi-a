About Breakout

Breakout is a brick breaker game. Bounce the ball off your paddle to smash a wall of bricks. Clear the wall and a new, faster one appears. You get 3 balls.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser.

Tips

  • Hit the ball with the edge of the paddle to send it at a sharper angle.
  • Try to break a hole at the side and get the ball above the bricks.
  • Each wall is faster, and bricks are worth more points on later walls.
  • Press P to pause.

How to play

  • Move the paddle to keep the ball in play. You get 3 balls.
  • Every brick you hit breaks and scores points.
  • Clear the whole wall and a faster one appears.

Controls

Mouse or arrow keys on a computer, drag on a phone.
