About Higher or Lower

Higher or Lower is a quick quiz game. The left card shows something and its number, like the population of Iceland or the top speed of a cheetah. The right card shows something else from the same topic, with its number hidden. Guess if the hidden number is higher or lower. Get it right and the card slides over, and a new one comes in. Topics change every few guesses, and new questions come every week.

  • Free to play, with no download and no account.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Your best score is saved in your browser, and there is a top 10 for this week and for all time.

Tips

  • Think about how big the left number is. Very big numbers are usually followed by smaller ones, and very small ones by bigger ones.
  • Both cards are always from the same topic, so compare like with like.
  • There is no timer. Take your time.

How to play

  • Read the two cards. The left one shows its number.
  • Tap HIGHER if you think the right card's number is bigger, or LOWER if it is smaller.
  • Each right guess is 1 point. One wrong guess and the run is over.
  • If both numbers are the same, both answers are right.

Controls

Tap or click HIGHER or LOWER. On a keyboard, press the up arrow for higher and the down arrow for lower.

Questions

How is the score counted?

You get 1 point for every right guess in a row. One wrong guess ends the run.

What if both numbers are the same?

Then both HIGHER and LOWER are right.

Do the questions change?

Yes. New questions come every week. Within one run, a card is not shown twice until every card has been used.
